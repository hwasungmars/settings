# User Paper Triage Preferences

Standing preferences for the `research` skill.

## Primary Research Scope
- **Post-Training & RL**: Core interest in foundational RL algorithms for LLMs (off-policy stabilisation, advantage estimation, credit assignment, critic reduction, direct preference learning, test-time compute, reasoning distillation).
- **Inference Optimisation**: Speculative decoding/drafting, KV cache compression and eviction, chunked prefill, attention memory efficiency, and serving throughput.
- **Signal-to-Noise Standard**: Rigorous standard for "breakthrough" (top 1% algorithmic and mathematical innovations). Pure benchmark churn, minor prompt engineering wrappers, and heuristic hyperparameter recipes should be filtered out.

## Evaluation Priority
- **Algorithm Over Brand**: While major research organisations publish significant work, evaluate all candidate papers strictly on their mathematical soundness, theoretical novelty, and empirical validation rather than institutional prestige.
- **Verification Rule**: Verify key claims and equations directly against the paper text before declaring a Tier 1 breakthrough.

## Delivery Preferences
- **Default Review Window**: Past 7 days (for recurring Friday reviews).
- **Format**: Concise, high-density technical analysis focusing on the failure mode, the mathematical remedy, and practical implications.
